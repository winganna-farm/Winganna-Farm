Meet the Animals

Meet the Animals

Friendly Faces Around Every Corner

One of the highlights of staying at Winganna Farm is meeting the wonderful animals that call the farm home, whether they're greeting you at the fence or wandering through the paddocks.

Every animal has its own personality, and guests of all ages enjoy spending time getting to know them throughout their stay.

Feathered Friends

Chickens & Guinea Fowl

We have plenty of Chickens on the farm who love to meet the guests. Every afternoon you are welcome to come and join us as we feed them and you may even be able to collect some eggs.

Our Guinea Fowl are allways wondering around the farm, protecting the other animals safe from the occasional snake in the summer.

Farm Favourites

Pig, Goats & Sheep

Our Goats are always a favourite with guests. Their playful personalities and inquisitive nature make them some of the most memorable animals on the farm.

Our Pig 'Wilma' is a top attraction during animal feeding time. She is extremely friendly and enjoys socialising with guests whenever possible.

Although our sheep prefer to keep their distance, everyone enjoys saying hello and giving them a feed. Sometimes you will spot young lambs with their mothers during Spring.

Gentle Giants

Alpacas, Donkey, Steer & Horses

Our Alpacas are an inquisitive bunch. Although they prefer to keep their distance, they may come over if you bring some of their faviourite snacks. Our Donkey enjoys their company but is always willing to say hello especially if you come prepared with some carrots.

Our Steer 'Rusty' is quite literally a gentle giant. Although he weighs just over 500kg, all he wants to do is meet new people and enjoy your company.

Our horses mostly keep to themselves but will come over if you have a bit of food. It is always an amazing sight to watch them gallop into the sunset.

Our Pet Policy

Bringing your own pets

We are a pet friendly farm stay here at Winganna. Wadella Cottage has a fully enclosed fence and a yard for the dogs to run around in and enjoy.
