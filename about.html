About Winganna Farm

Our Story

A Place to Slow Down

Winganna Farm is a peaceful country retreat where guests can step away from busy everyday life and reconnect with nature. Surrounded by open paddocks, friendly farm animals and beautiful rural scenery, every stay offers the opportunity to relax, explore and enjoy the simple pleasures of country living.

Whether you're travelling as a family, a couple or a group of friends, we hope every guest leaves with lasting memories of fresh country air, unforgettable sunsets and time well spent together.

The Property

Experience Country Living

Set amongst picturesque countryside, Winganna Farm offers guests plenty of space to relax, explore and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Open paddocks and beautiful rural views create a welcoming environment throughout every season.

Spend your days wandering around the property, enjoying the fresh air, watching the animals or simply taking time to appreciate the slower pace of country life. Whether you're seeking adventure or quiet relaxation, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

The Local Area

Welcome to Peel

Peel is a small village located 15 km north of Bathurst on the road to Sofala and Mudgee. The area around the village of Peel began as a mining venture with the Ebenezer Quartz Mine launched in 1849. Prospectors from around the world came to Peel and looked for gold along Winburndale Rivulet and Clear Creek. There used to be seven pubs in Peel but now either they have been restored as homes or been demolished.

Peel was a common stop during the gold rush as prospectors and adventurers travelled to Hill End and Sofala to continue or begin their search for gold. It is a 73 kilometre journey from Bathurst to Hill End and takes just over an hour by car. Back then, it took several days, so people would also stop at Peel because there are two water ways, the Winburndale Rivulet and Clear Creek.
