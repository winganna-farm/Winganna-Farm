About Winganna Farm

Winganna Farm is your next family farm getaway. 50 acres of land just 20 minutes from Bathurst CBD. You will stay in a historical cottage as the only guests on the property. You can meet our friendly animals, including goats, chooks, sheep, the pig, the donkey, and many more. The Winburndale Rivulet is a cool spot in the summer, located on the property.

Life on the Farm

Stay

Your Country Escape

Built in 1854 this 3brm secluded cottage on our farm stay is where you can enjoy the peacefulness of the country with all the comforts of the modern day and only 20min from Bathurst city centre.
Relaxing on the wrap around balcony you will hear the sounds of the rivulet below, the birdlife and farm animals which we invite you to come and meet and feed in the afternoons. Inside is everything you need including the comfort of a fireplace or A/C in the summer.
